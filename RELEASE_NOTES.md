Pauso 0.15.1 — Music browser

- Switch between Sounds, Songs, Folders and Playlists from a compact menu.
- Add a whole music folder at once and see import progress.
- Search and sort the open collection.
- Tap a track to play in place. The mini player stays visible across Music views, and the current track is highlighted.
